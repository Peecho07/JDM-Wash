# JDM Wash LLC: Website Demo

Built on spec by tsKaizen. Prices, services, plans, service area, contact info and the founders' story come from the current jdm-wash-llc-ibxr5e30.durable.site.

## What changed and why it brings in more customers

| Current site | New demo | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Lives on a durable.site subdomain | Ready to run on jdmwashllc.com (the domain you already use for email) | A real domain looks established and ranks better on Google than a builder subdomain. |
| Testimonials that customers may read as fake (one is attributed to "Jennifer Lopez", and names differ between pages) | Removed. Section is ready for real Google reviews once you collect a few | One suspicious review makes people doubt all of them. Real reviews with real names convert. |
| Monthly plans and one-time prices on separate pages | Both on one page, with a Sedan vs SUV toggle that updates plan prices instantly | Customers compare options in seconds instead of clicking around. Plans are recurring revenue, so they get their own spotlight. |
| No phone number on the homepage | Call and Book buttons pinned to the bottom of every phone screen, plus click-to-text | Same-day customers want to call now. Make it one tap. |
| Same-day service mentioned in passing | It's the headline badge, the main button and the booking flow | It's your biggest edge over shops with a 3 day wait. |
| Generic AI-builder look | Custom dark design with a JDM gauge cluster that shows the 4400 PSI washer | Matches the car culture brand and looks different from every other detailer in Kissimmee. |
| Booking request says only "call or use the form" | Booking form asks for year, make, model, service and ZIP, and every package has a "Book this" that prefills it | Fewer back-and-forth texts before a job is confirmed. |

## Before launch

- Add 6 to 10 real before/after photos (the current site has none of your actual work). A gallery section can be added in an hour.
- Ask your last 10 customers for Google reviews, then link them on the site.
- Confirm Sunday hours (demo shows Closed, since the current site lists Monday to Saturday).

## Technical notes

- Plain HTML, CSS and JS. No build step. Deploys to Netlify by drag and drop.
- Booking form uses Netlify Forms. Submissions show in the Netlify dashboard and can email or text you.
